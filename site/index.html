01 · Who we are

One partner from drawing to handover.

Piping work fails at the interfaces: between the fabrication shop and the site, between the installer and the inspector, between the work done and the paperwork that proves it. We close those gaps by running all of it ourselves.

Our engineers, skilled craftsmen, quality controllers and project managers work as one team on mechanical and piping projects of every size, from small installations to large industrial projects.

02 · Services

Everything a piping project needs, under one contract.

Hire us for the full scope or for a single discipline. Either way, you get one point of contact and one standard of work.

Project management

Planning, scheduling, procurement follow-up and subcontractor coordination for mechanical and piping projects, with one project lead answerable to you.

  • Schedule & progress reporting
  • Cost and change control

Prefabrication

Spools are cut, fitted and welded off-site under controlled conditions, so site work is faster, cleaner and shutdown windows stay short.

  • Spool fabrication to isometrics
  • Less hot work on site

Construction & installation

New piping systems, modifications and tie-ins to live plants, including pipe racks, supports and steel structures, delivered to schedule and budget.

  • New-build and revamps
  • Steel structures & supports

Quality control

Inspection and test plans, material traceability and weld monitoring at every stage, so every joint meets the code and your project specification.

  • ITPs and hold points
  • Material & weld traceability

Inspection & testing

Dimensional, visual and pressure testing that catches problems before they reach commissioning, protecting the integrity and safety of your system.

  • Pressure & leak testing
  • Punch lists to close-out

Document control

Complete, audit-ready records: certificates, weld logs, test reports and as-built drawings, compiled into a handover dossier your client will accept first time.

  • Handover & MDR dossiers
  • As-built documentation

03 · How we work

A clear path from enquiry to handover.

You always know what stage your project is in, who is responsible and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Scope & quote

    We review your drawings, line lists and specs, ask the right questions and send a clear quotation.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Schedule, inspection and test plan, material take-off and safety plan, agreed before work starts.

  3. 03

    Prefabricate

    Spools are built off-site, checked and labelled, ready for fast installation.

  4. 04

    Install & test

    Site installation, supports and tie-ins, followed by inspection and pressure testing.

  5. 05

    Hand over

    Punch list closed, as-builts updated and a complete dossier delivered.

07 · FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Something else? Call +31 6 285 01 868.

Which industries do you work in?

We build and manage piping and mechanical projects in the oil and gas industry, petrochemical plants and refineries. Our safety management system is certified to SCCP:2011, the standard for contractors working in the petrochemical industry and refineries.

Which certifications do you hold?

ISO 9001:2015 for quality management (registration Q-29567/0) and SCCP:2011 for safety management (registration CC-00054/0), both issued by Quality Austria.

Can I contract a single service, such as prefabrication or inspection?

Yes. Each discipline can be contracted on its own or combined into a complete package managed by one project lead.

What should I send to get a quote?

The project location, the scope (for example isometrics, P&IDs or a line list), required materials and standards, and your target start date. We reply with questions or a quotation.
